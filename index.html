Chromify's WebP Tool

Slice a 256 px sprite sheet into frames, lay your own 256 px image on top, and export one looping animated WebP.

1 / 1

Sprite sheet

Every frame is 256 × 256. Cells are read left to right, then top to bottom. Empty cells at the end are skipped.

Overlay image

A 256 × 256 PNG or WebP with a transparent background works best. Other sizes are fitted inside 256 × 256.

Speed

fps

Export

The file loops forever and keeps transparency. Extra quality uses a near-maximum WebP encoder setting to help retain detail through later social-media recompression; platforms may still recompress it.
